    Our Kamarucho adventure story!

    Atmosphere, atmosphere, atmosphere! Kamurocho offers a neon-drenched vibe that cannot be found elsewhere. The Millennium Tower for example. This skyscraper is a sight to behold, and seeing it up close is more imposing than we've ever imagined. Want to enjoy a nice and cozy bowl of ramen? Well, Kamurocho will never fail you.

    One of the most chaotic patrols we've done so far..

    As a district in a sprawling metropolis, Kamurocho with its narrow streets has to compete not only with other entertainment districts but with entire virtual worlds. There are, for instance, no quiet, open fields.

    The advantage is that a trip to Kamurocho benefits from a dense concentration of vibrant experiences in a few city blocks.

    The advantage is that a trip to Kamurocho benefits from a dense concentration of vibrant experiences in a few city blocks, offering possibilities for all kinds of action at any hour, day or night, although primarily after sunset. The offers of entertainment as well as underground activities are manifold, but unfortunately the constant risk of a random street fight is high, and this can discourage the faint-hearted from lingering. Despite this major drawback, legions of players from all over the world dive in every year.

    Disclamer

    The COVID-19 pandemic has dealt a severe blow to the tourism business in Yakuza. Although it is a minor position in the Japan export balance, it is nevertheless of considerable importance for those regions of the country that attract domestic and foreign visitors. As a consequence of the lockdown measures, combined with close borders and travel bans, tourism collapsed and only briefly and partly recovered in summer. Its future is uncertain and depends on people’s (future guests’) attitudes and decisions as much as on the economy, political measures and, of course, the progress of the pandemic.
